This site brings together three things: reusable AI skills, introductions to launched services, and selected notes from building and learning.

**Skills** will include instructions, setup notes, examples, and downloadable packages. Some skills need supporting tools or code; the page will describe those requirements.

**Services** will explain what a launched service does and where to use it. A project under development will not be presented as a service you can use today.

**Notes** will describe questions, methods, checked results, and limitations. A successful experiment and a reliable operating workflow are different milestones.

The first skills are being prepared. No service is listed yet. New entries will be added when they are ready to share.

I’m Hosang Kim. This is a personal project, and the public material here is selected separately from employer-specific work.
