# Voice: dropkit writing (English, canonical)

Lab-notebook tone. Not the product name.

Public ledger. Learn-in-public lab notebook. Not a magazine, not a rundown, not a GPU shop diary.

## Tone
- Short paragraphs. One claim per paragraph when possible.
- Numbers, failures, and URLs visible. Adjectives only if they change a decision.
- Easy to read. No press-release voice. No “excited to announce”.
- Do not invent. If the harvest lacks a number, write “not in sources”.
- Do not bias to only Silicon Valley or only China.

## Shape
- Deep cut: systems, infra, papers, open-weights, chips, agent stacks. Show the mechanism.
- Scoreboard: a table is the body. Two lines of prose max around it.
- End with three practitioner lines: act / watch / ignore — only order-robust items.

## Forbidden
- Hype lists without names, dates, or URLs
- Translating this voice into Korean in the same file
- Mentioning the factory, prompts, or model names as the story
